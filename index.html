About me

I develop primarily on the backend – from the API through authentication to deployment. Accessibility isn't an add-on for me, it's a mindset: I work terminal-first with a screen reader and know from daily practice which tools and workflows actually hold up.

I'm driven by analytical thinking, curiosity, and resilience. My focus areas are Rust and Java, web development, DevOps, cloud, and building transparent, auditable systems.
